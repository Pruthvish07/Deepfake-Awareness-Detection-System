5-Point Quick Verification Checklist

  • 1
    Unnatural Blink Frequency Biological humans blink every 3–6 seconds. Synthetic face-swaps frequently exhibit completely static eyelids or irregular jitter.
  • 2
    Jawline & Hairline Boundary Blending Inspect where the face meets the neck or collar. Deepfake algorithms struggle to seamlessly blend skin tone gradients across edges.
  • 3
    Asymmetric Pupil Catchlights Look at the reflection in both eyes. In genuine photos, reflection matches the single light source. In GANs, pupils often reflect different shapes.
  • 4
    Voice Frequency & Breath Gaps Neural voice clones often clip frequencies above 8 kHz and lack biological throat resonance and breathing pauses between words.
  • 5
    Fine Structural Symmetry (Teeth & Ears) Generative diffusion models struggle to render individually defined teeth, often creating a solid white bar or asymmetric earring geometries.
