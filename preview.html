🔍 5-Point Quick Verification Checklist

  • 1. Blink Patterns: Real people blink every 3–6 seconds. AI face-swaps often have frozen or twitchy blinks.
  • 2. Face Boundary Blending: Watch the jawline, collar, and hairline for hazy or blurred transitions.
  • 3. Pupil Catchlights: Check if reflections in both eyes match the room's actual light source.
  • 4. Audio Harmonics: Neural voice clones frequently clip audio frequencies above 8 kHz and lack breath sounds.
  • 5. Teeth & Jewelry: Diffusion models struggle with fine repeated textures like individual teeth and earring symmetry.
